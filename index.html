BEFORE YOU BEGIN
01

Choose a safe viewpoint. Never approach an active landslide, unstable slope, or cliff edge to get a better angle.

02

Move around the scene, not across the hazard. Stay on stable, authorized ground and keep a consistent distance where possible.

03

Five is a starting minimum. Complex terrain, weak texture, or occlusion may need more photos. This guide cannot confirm reconstruction quality.
